~/about.md

        

          

            I am currently studying
            Computer Systems Engineering at
            Universidad Autónoma de Aguascalientes. I am
            interested in software development in general — web development,
            backend, and databases — and, increasingly, in understanding what
            happens under the tools I use every day.
          


          

            I am self-taught out of necessity and curiosity: when I want to
            learn something new, I do not start by reading theory; I start by
            building and experimenting. That is how I have learned to work with
            PostgreSQL directly from psql, manage my development
            environment on Linux, and solve problems by understanding how things
            work rather than simply following tutorials.
          


          

            Right now, I am strengthening my JavaScript and
            TypeScript skills and exploring low-level
            programming — memory, processes, concurrency, and how an operating
            system works internally.
